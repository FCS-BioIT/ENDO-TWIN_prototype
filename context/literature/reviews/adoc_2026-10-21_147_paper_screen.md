## How to use this index

1. Cross-match these references with `context/literature/references.tsv`, `context/literature/active_evidence_manifest.tsv`, and the September 163-paper audit before calling a paper new or missing.
2. For a claimed model edge or rule, locate the original PDF, verify its exact figure, material, intervention/comparator, compartment, species, molecular readout, and **independent experimental unit**. Record evidence in the *existing* canonical schema after governance authorization, not in this table.
3. Do not infer quantitative MaBoSS rates, attachment competence, human embryo causality, or endpoint gates from the `A` tier alone.
4. The decision-making summary and contradictions are in the companion mechanistic dossier. Its proposals are **not** implementation authorization.
