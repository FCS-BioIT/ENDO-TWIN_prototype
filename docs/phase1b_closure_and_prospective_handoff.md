# Phase-1B closure audit and prospective `dropout_e2` handoff

**Status: Phase 1B is BLOCKED under the current claim contract (audited
2026-09-24 against `adoc-literature-superset-v0.5.1`).** This status is derived,
not chosen. All four exit criteria are unmet, and for none of them can the
current plan supply everything needed:

- E1, molecular directions: also needs TI-04 de-aliasing, which is deferred, and
  an untreated PAEP arm, which does not exist.
- E2, adhesion agreement.
- E3, a predicted held-out perturbation.
- E4, fragile-assumption independence: also needs threshold evidence and an
  alternative mifepristone encoding.

The frozen prospective experiment advances E1 and E4, closes neither, and does
not touch E2 or E3.

## 1. How the status is derived

The rule is fixed in code (`phase1b_closure.OVERALL_RULE`) and recomputed by a
test from the committed TSV. It reads the four exit-gate rows only:

- **CLOSED** if every exit-gate row is `SATISFIED` or `NOT_APPLICABLE`;
- otherwise **BLOCKED** if any unmet row has `unblock_route = NO_SCHEDULED_ROUTE`;
- otherwise **PARTIAL**.

The roadmap-item rows are the work that feeds the gate. They are reported but do
not gate on their own. A test drives the rule to all three outcomes.

Every curated status that rests on a computed fact is tied to it. The premises
are listed in `phase1b_closure.EXPECTED_PREMISES` and are all exact fixed-point
enumerations or contract reads on v0.5.1. If one moves, the build raises `AUDIT_PREMISE_MOVED` instead of
publishing a status that has lost its ground. An example is the model ceasing to
give the mifepristone arm P = 0.

The judgements worth stating in words:

- **R1 has no fitting, by policy.** `config/calibration.yaml` carries no
  quantitative objective. Adhesion is `diagnostic_ordinal`. The only filter
  based on data is OM-001. The data in hand cannot separate the pre-declared SEC
  alternatives, which is why the prospective experiment exists.
- **R3b is NOT_APPLICABLE, which is not SATISFIED.** No rate is declared. The
  network is acyclic given its free variables, and this is computed. Every
  retained claim is a fixed-point claim, so none depends on rates. The row turns
  applicable as soon as any transient, finite-horizon or graded claim is made.
- **R4a is NOT_APPLICABLE because the roadmap's own precondition fails.** The
  mifepristone direction was used in construction (LE002 and LE038 cite
  SOFIA-HT-002).
- **E1 is partial, and circular where it holds.** OM-001 and OM-002 are the
  model's only two measured nodes and also its filter, and both are binary at the
  fixed point. OM-001 is not the registered HT-vs-untreated claim. The
  IGFBP-1-before-PRL onset order cannot be expressed, because `S_PRL` and
  `S_IGFBP1` share one rule.
- **E4 is not demonstrated.** No latent assignment inverts the ordinal adhesion
  claim: untreated and mifepristone are OFF in every weighted assignment. The
  strict ordering holds only in expectation. But the molecular behaviour rests on
  the committed `SEC-a` rule, which is an open fork. The mifepristone encoding is
  complete antagonism. The E_PAEP threshold is implicit. The AC01 alias verdict
  depends on the D1 interpretive call. No one-at-a-time or global sensitivity
  exists.

## 3. Mifepristone: the exact validation claim

**There is none.** In repository terms:

- **Direction:** not held out. The antagonism was encoded from the same study, so
  a reduction was guaranteed. The roadmap item's precondition is false, and the
  row is `NOT_APPLICABLE`.
- **Magnitude:** held out, and the hold-out is enforced in code. On v0.5.1 the
  exact fixed-point probability is zero against an observed 1/8, so the arm is
  **`structurally_contradicted` and `quantitatively_unscorable_for_ranking`**.
  The row is `NOT_SATISFIED`.
- **What this is not.** It is not a successful validation. It is not a failed
  validation that falsifies the maternal model either: per
  `config/calibration.yaml`, the observation "cannot identify or justify a
  particular maternal-rule change". No maternal rule was altered.

## 4. Sensitivity: what exists and what does not

`sensitivity_inventory.tsv` has one row per kind. The short version:

- **Run:**
  - seed/trajectory reproducibility (v0.5.0 endpoint; the v0.5.1 transient screen of U5b);
  - horizon stability (v0.3/v0.4);
  - the U6b structural rule family.
- **Partial:**
  - initial states: an exhaustive latent Boolean enumeration and the KLF12 prior sweep, both on v0.4. The relay priors were never swept.
  - alternative rules: exact structural evaluation, never propagated.
- **Not run:**
  - rates (not applicable at the fixed point);
  - thresholds (forbidden to choose from the same data);
  - any uncertainty-ensemble or global propagation.

The roadmap promises one-at-a-time *and* global sensitivity over thresholds,
rates, initial states and rules. That has not been produced at the required
scope. This unit records the gap and does not run a late campaign. No retained
claim needs one, because none is a magnitude. The declared uncertainty sets it
would need do not exist yet.

## 5. The prospective handoff

The frozen design is `docs/stage3_experiment_selection.md` §4, §5, §9 and §12,
frozen 2026-09-17. It is carried as it stands, except where the owner addenda
below supersede it (the unit, SPP1):

- 3 planned source-pair blocks as the unit (the frozen text says "donors"; SPB1);
- days 0–2 E2-only priming;
- days 2–6 minus-E2 (MPA 1 µM + cAMP 0.25 mM + XAV939 10 µM), against a
  full-cocktail comparator;
- one chip per source-pair block per condition, plus a technical duplicate of the
  minus-E2 arm in the same block if capacity permits;
- PAEP from the top outlet, IGFBP-1 and PRL from the bottom outlet, at day 3 and
  day 6;
- a day-2 E2-only reference and a pre-cocktail PAEP aliquot;
- phase-contrast integrity imaging in both channels at days 2, 4 and 6;
- no embryo;
- the optional SPP1 assay is not exercised in this run (SPP1X).

The generator requires the frozen phrases behind these items to appear verbatim
in the frozen text, and renders them into the handoff; the list above
paraphrases them. It asserts that the handoff's own dose, day,
outlet and replication statements agree with those quotes. Doses are
cross-checked against `config/protocols.yaml`, the band limits are recomputed
from the canonical protein table, and the §5 decision tree is copied cell for
cell. The remaining bench prose is a restatement governed by the quotes and the
addendum. A tampered phrase or a divergent handoff value stops the build, and
tests show that both do.

Two templates are generated for the bench:
`prospective_allocation_manifest_template.tsv` and
`prospective_assay_metadata_template.tsv`. Both read
`REQUIRED_PRE_RUN_MANIFEST` until real IDs and limits exist. That is an
operational prerequisite, not an open interpretation question, and no treatment
begins and nothing is classified until the templates are complete.

Two readings in the implementation are the author's, not the owner's:

- A non-detect or censored PAEP minus-E2 value is also not classified (it gets
  the same non-class labels as IGFBP-1), because MS1 lets only `observed` enter
  the classifier.
- A reference control that is non-detect or censored is
  CONTROL_STATE_NOT_CONFIRMED. A missing control, or one with a technical
  failure, gives that label for the dependent call.

**What the result can and cannot be.** The `dropout_e2` result will
re-adjudicate the SEC fork, which is model construction. It therefore cannot
also be E3's held-out validation (AGENTS.md: do not tune on the same held-out
outcomes used for validation). Its first outcome row licenses sustained E2
dependence of the epithelial secretory programme, not the mechanism: `SEC-a` and
`SEC-e` predict identically on both usable assays.

## 6. After the data arrive

1. Ingest into a declared derived table. `data/` stays immutable. No band, rule
   or threshold changes.
2. Apply the P0 order per source-pair block. Record every intermediate call and
   its reason.
3. Map each valid block pattern to the frozen §5 row, and record disagreement
   between blocks and between days explicitly.
4. Re-evaluate the SEC fork and AC01 in a new unit.
5. Only then decide whether a new executable model version is licensed. Neither
   the audit nor the handoff prescribes the rule the data must produce.
