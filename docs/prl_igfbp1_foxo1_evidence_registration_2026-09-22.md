## 1. The one disposition that moved: `S_FOXO1`

`WAIT_FOR_EXPERIMENT` → **`RETAIN_CATALOG_ONLY`**, first failing criterion
`5(2)_determinant_activity_vs_abundance` → **`5(4)_delta_and_alias`**,
`unblock_locus` unchanged at `ADOC_RUNNABLE`. Ledger row `PIF-001`.

**This is an evidence advance recorded as a vocabulary demotion, and it must be
read that way.** §5(2) asked for a determinant of the variable the node names —
FOXO1 *nuclear* activity — rather than of FOXO1 mRNA or total protein. Labied
2006 (`REF-078`/`PDF-050`) supplies exactly that: FOXO1 nuclear versus
cytoplasmic distribution measured **as localisation**, by confocal
immunofluorescence *and* by immunoblot of separated cytosolic and nuclear
fractions, in primary human endometrial stromal cells, with MPA driving
cytoplasmic retention via PI3K/Akt (LY294002 reverses it) and MPA withdrawal
restoring nuclear FOXO1 within 1 h. MPA and cAMP are both agents of
`COND-FULL-COCKTAIL`. So §5(2) clears, and the first failure moves later — which
in this ladder produces a *weaker-sounding* label for a *stronger* evidence
state.

**Criterion (4) is where it now fails**, and (4) is a conjunction: a measured
increase in distinct Boolean functions **and** a named alias class broken. Every
encoding the evidence supports fails one conjunct or the other. Re-verified in
this session by exhaustive enumeration of all 2¹⁹ = 524,288 assignments of the
committed `models/superset/adoc-literature-superset-v0.5.0/network.bnd` (59
labels → **35** distinct Boolean functions, **19** free variables).

Measured **two ways**, because a candidate node that nothing reads changes
nothing downstream. *Unwired* is the node added and read by no rule; *wired* is
the staged form, with `S_IGFBP1 = S_DECIDUALIZED and S_FOXO1`.

| candidate encoding | unwired | wired | alias class broken (wired) | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `S_FOXO1 = S_PGR and S_PKA` | 35 → 35 | 35 → 35 | no | identical to `S_DECIDUALIZED`; vacuous |
| `S_FOXO1 = S_PKA` | 35 → 35 | 35 → 35 | no | Δ 0 |
| `S_FOXO1 = S_PKA and not IN_MIFEPRISTONE` | 35 → 36 | 35 → 36 | **no** — the conjunct is absorbed, `S_PRL` and `S_IGFBP1` stay identical | gains, breaks nothing |
| `S_FOXO1 = S_PKA and not S_PGR` | 35 → 36 | 35 → 37 | yes | but makes `S_IGFBP1` **unsatisfiable**; refuted by `OM-002`/`OM-016`; excluded under §3.3 |

The same enumeration confirms `S_DECIDUALIZED` **is exactly** `S_PGR and S_PKA`,
which is why the first row is vacuous rather than merely inert — and why the last
row is identically false once `S_IGFBP1` reads it.

**Correction to the external analysis package.** `MODEL_IMPLICATIONS.md` §4 gives
`S_PKA and not S_PGR` as "Δ +1 … 35 → 37" and as making `S_IGFBP1` unsatisfiable,
without saying that both hold only for the *wired* form. Unwired it is 35 → 36
and `S_IGFBP1` stays satisfiable, because nothing reads the new node. The
adjudication is unaffected — the encoding is excluded under §3.3 either way — but
the figures are wrong by one function as stated, and are qualified here.

`unblock_locus` stays `ADOC_RUNNABLE` for a **new** reason. What is missing is no
longer a determinant; it is an ADOC-addressable **input** that separates
`S_FOXO1` from `S_DECIDUALIZED`. The discriminating measurement is still one ADOC
can make — nuclear/cytoplasmic FOXO1 fractionation — but **it has to be read on a
new single-agent dropout arm, not on the arms that exist.**
`config/protocols.yaml`'s `component_identifiability_note` is explicit: *"The
design has no single-agent arm, so no effect may be attributed to MPA, cAMP or
XAV939 individually"*, and `IN_XAV939` is additionally a zero-degree node, so the
simulated response to this protocol contains no XAV939 contribution at all.
`docs/design_alias/node_design_classes.tsv` says the same thing structurally:
`IN_E2`, `IN_PROGESTIN`, `IN_XAV939`, `IN_cAMP` and `S_PKA` are **one
design-equivalence class** under the four planned arms.

Labied tested MPA and cAMP and neither E2 nor XAV939, and `S_DECIDUALIZED` reads
neither — it is exactly `S_PGR and S_PKA`. So E2 and XAV939 are precisely the two
cocktail agents that could separate `S_FOXO1` from `S_DECIDUALIZED`, and the
candidate arms are **full cocktail minus E2** or **minus XAV939**. The relation
between them is conditional, not conjunctive: **either** arm creates the required
new state *if* stromal FOXO1 localisation responds to that agent, and which one
does is exactly what is unknown. So a null in one does **not** clear the other,
and neither can be read off existing material. Minus-E2 is the one already
adjudicated and frozen for other reasons
(`docs/stage3_experiment_selection.md` §12), so it is the one to carry the
fractionation readout first; minus-XAV939 remains open if that returns a null.

### 1.1 Labied 2006: exactly what it did and did not test

This correction matters because the brief for this increment, and the external
analysis bundle, both stated that "the exact ADOC MPA+cAMP combination was not
tested". **The paper's own Figure 3 contradicts that.** Figure 3A is 8-br-cAMP
*in the presence or absence of MPA*; Figure 3B fractionates the same conditions;
Figure 3C applies LY294002 to `cAMP/MPA`-treated cells. The combination is the
paper's central comparison.

What is *not* tested, stated precisely:

- **No E2 arm and no XAV939 arm anywhere.** `COND-FULL-COCKTAIL` is E2 10 nM +
  MPA 1 µM + cAMP 0.25 mM + XAV939 10 µM; Labied is 8-br-cAMP 0.5 mM ± MPA 1 µM.
  Two of four agents, and a different cAMP analogue at twice the concentration.
- **The ADOC condition identity is itself unratified.**
  `config/adoc_outlet_conditions.tsv` records `COND-FULL-COCKTAIL` as
  `UNRATIFIED: confirm with the collaborator whether the progestin was MPA and
  whether cAMP was included`. So even the two-agent overlap is provisional.
- **Attenuation, not abolition.** Under 8-br-cAMP **plus** MPA the paper reports
  *discrete residual nuclear FOXO1 foci*, arrowheaded in Figure 3A. A binary
  progestin veto on nuclear FOXO1 is therefore over-strict against the paper's
  own figure — which is independently why `S_FOXO1 = S_PKA and not S_PGR` is
  excluded under §3.3 rather than admitted under (4).
- **PRL and IGFBP-1 are not measured.** Labied says nothing about the alias.

Vasquez 2015 (`PIF-015`) reproduces the residual-nuclear-fraction observation
under E2 + MPA + db-cAMP, which is the closest condition in this corpus to the
ADOC cocktail.

## 2. Claims that became stronger

- **`S_PRL` and `S_IGFBP1` are biologically non-identical.** Ujvari 2017
  (`PIF-004`) is the cleanest instance: a FOXO1 *activity* inhibitor collapses
  IGFBP1 (FC 0.13, p<0.05) while PRL is unchanged (FC 1.1, NS), and insulin
  lowers IGFBP1 dose-dependently while *raising* PRL 3.0-fold. Buzzio 2006
  (`PIF-003`) moves them in opposite directions under one knockdown. Kane 2010
  (`PIF-014`) separates them by mechanism — SMAD4 knockdown abrogates the TGFβ1
  effect on PRL but does not fully restore IGFBP-1.
- **FOXO1 activity is localisation, not abundance**, now on two independent
  primary-human fractionation results (Labied 2006, Vasquez 2015) plus a third
  mechanism converging on the same route (Kang 2022, PI3K/Akt).
- **FOXO1 licenses PR genomic action** (Vasquez 2015) — a step the committed
  model represents nowhere.
- **The committed `E_MUC1_BARRIER` rule is contradicted by primary human in vivo
  evidence** with the hormonal intervention, the embryo and a spatial readout
  together (Meseguer 2001, `PIF-008`).
- **Kane 2010 is now resolvable.** It was cited in repository prose five times —
  `config/coupling_adjudication.tsv` row `S_PRL`, `docs/coupling_adjudication.md`
  §3.2 and four sites in `docs/v05_plan_review.md` — with no row in
  `references.tsv`, `pdf_manifest.tsv`, `citation_verification.tsv`, any
  `*.primary-verification.tsv` or `context/evidence-register.tsv`.

## 3. Claims that were weakened, excluded or corrected

### 3.1 Corrections to the external analysis bundle and to the draft ledger

Each was found by reading the primary source in this session, and each is carried
in the relevant `PIF-*` row. Four are defects in the external package itself
(items 2 and the second half of 4, plus 5 and 9, i.e. `MODEL_IMPLICATIONS.md`
§§1.1, 2.1, 3 and 4); the other five entered the **draft ledger** downstream of it
— items 1, 3, 7 and 8, and Gipson's siRNA-versus-shRNA wording. The package
already carried the correct Li 2007 PMID in `SEARCH_INTERFACE.md`, so the draft
picked the wrong one from an internally inconsistent source. All nine are written
back to the package as `ERRATA.md`, which states the same split.

1. **Labied 2006 *did* test MPA together with cAMP** — §1.1. The draft ledger's
   `what_is_NOT_supported` asserted the opposite. The adjudication is unchanged,
   because (4) fails on structure; the stated reason is corrected.
2. **Gipson 2008 does not report "MUC1 present in 12/12".** MUC1 is on the
   *luminal surface* in 12/12 receptive samples but on **uterodomes in only 4 of
   12** (weak/intermittent in 3, ciliated cells only in 2); MUC16 is absent from
   uterodomes in 12/12. Quoting the two as a matched 12/12-versus-12/12 pair
   compares different structures. `MODEL_IMPLICATIONS.md` §1.1 and the draft
   ledger both did so.
3. **Li 2007's PMID was wrong.** The draft carried `17711858`, which is Row et
   al., *The MIT domain of UBPY…*, a different J Biol Chem 2007 article. The
   correct PMID, resolved by DOI at NCBI, is **17711857**.
4. **Li 2007's human hormone mixture includes cAMP.** It is 0.5 mM 8-bromo-cAMP
   + 1 µM P + 10 nM E (arms `EP`, `EPC`, `EPCB`), not "P + E" as the draft and
   the bundle both stated. The BMP2 addition therefore sits on top of a *closer*
   analogue of the ADOC cocktail than was thought, which strengthens rather than
   weakens the falsification of `S_BMP2 ≡ S_PGR`.
5. **Vasquez 2015's PRL result was misattributed.** The significant attenuation
   of IGFBP1, PRL and WNT4 (Figure 6B, days 3 and 6) is the **IRF4** knockdown.
   Under **FOXO1** knockdown the main text says only that IGFBP-1 and PRL are
   "differentially regulated" (Supplemental Figure 2) — *no direction is stated
   and there is no RT-qPCR validation for those two genes*, and that supplement
   is not in the held file. So Vasquez cannot serve as the "PRL down" arm of the
   three-way FOXO1→PRL sign conflict. The conflict is weaker than reported: what
   survives is Buzzio (PRL **up**, confounded by FOXO3A) against Ujvari (PRL
   **unchanged**, a null at n=6).
6. **Kim 2005 is held as an NIH Public Access author manuscript**, not the
   version of record. Its 18 recorded pages are manuscript pagination, not
   journal pages 833–839. Recorded in `rendering_read` and in `PIF-005`'s
   `verification_route`.
7. **Paule 2012's primary-cell arm uses a PC6 inhibitor (Poly R), not siRNA.**
   The stable siRNA knockdown is in HEC1A only.
8. **Zhang 2020's retraction banner is on 15 pages, not 17** (the PDF has 15).
9. **The `S_FOXO1` structural figures needed a wiring qualification** — §1's
   table and its note. `35 → 37` and "makes `S_IGFBP1` unsatisfiable" hold for the
   *wired* form only; unwired both are different. The adjudication is unaffected.
10. **`MODEL_IMPLICATIONS.md` §3's de-aliasing witness is refuted by `OM-002`** —
    §4 item 1. `S_IGFBP1 = S_DECIDUALIZED and not IN_XAV939` forces IGFBP-1 OFF in
    the hormone-treated arm, where the measured signal rises. The polarity is
    backwards, and the general claim it supported ("the barrier is evidential, not
    structural") is too strong.
11. **`MODEL_IMPLICATIONS.md` §2.3's absorption sentence states the opposite of its
    own result** — §4 item 2. The marker conjuncts change the output in 0 of
    524,288 assignments, i.e. they are absorbed in all of them.
12. **`MODEL_IMPLICATIONS.md` §8's "only 17 of 76" is a regex artifact** — §4
    item 9. The true prior figure is 9 of 76.

Items 10–12 were raised by an external review of `71505c2` (OpenAI GPT-5.6,
reading the pushed tree) and each was independently confirmed here before being
applied. Two more of its findings were about this repository's own text rather
than the package — a guard docstring that claimed to read four artifacts when it
reads three, and a self-contradictory sentence about which arms could discriminate
`S_FOXO1` — and are corrected in place at §1 and §7.

### 3.2 System and context limitations carried explicitly

- **Placental, despite endometrial wording.** Grinius 2006 (`PIF-002`) uses
  decidual fibroblasts dissected from the **chorionic layer of placenta**; Kim
  2005 (`PIF-005`) uses HuF cells from **decidua parietalis of term placental
  membranes**, which the abstract calls "human endometrial fibroblasts". Thapa
  2023 is the third paper in this class and is *not* registered here. None
  transfers to a non-pregnant ADOC twin without an argument.
- **Buzzio 2006's siRNA also silences FOXO3A**, by the authors' own statement, so
  the PRL rise is not attributable to FOXO1A loss alone.
- **Lynch 2009's load-bearing results are in HeLa.** Every reporter, deletion and
  co-immunoprecipitation result is a HeLa cotransfection; the endometrial cells
  used elsewhere are telomerase-immortalised (ATCC CRL-4003), not primary.
- **Kang 2022 is an endometriosis paper with a mouse in vivo arm.** The primary
  human cells are normal endometrium, but the clinical contrast and the in vivo
  decidual response are not transferable.
- **Zhang 2020 is RETRACTED** (10 September 2026; notice
  `10.3389/fcell.2026.1988449`). The notice states that Figure 6D α-tubulin
  control lanes 1–3 appear identical to Figure 7B lanes 2–4 — the SMAD1/SMAD5
  knockdown blots carrying the central claim — that the authors failed to provide
  a satisfactory explanation, and that **the authors do not agree to the
  retraction**. Independently of that, its premise fails: CRL-4003 plus
  first-trimester decidua held in E2 + P4 throughout, so no progestin-free human
  arm. **Do not cite it** even if a fresh search ranks it first; the refusal is
  machine-readable as `registration_decision = excluded_retracted`.
- **Wen 2013** (ADAMTS aggrecanases) carries an **Expression of Concern**
  (J Cell Mol Med 2025;29(14):e70755). It is **not** in this repository at all —
  no reference row, no manifest row — so this is a prophylactic warning for the
  next search, not a defect being repaired.

### 3.3 `SOFIA-STR-001` — corrected, with the source's wording preserved

The row transcribed the manuscript's summary: *"PRL and IGFBP-1 were undetectable
day 0 and increased at days 3 and 6"*. That is **true for PRL** (0/6 detected at
day 0 against a 10 pg/mL limit) and **false for IGFBP-1**: the canonical workbook
`input_tables/adoc_secreted_proteins.tsv` detects basal stromal IGFBP-1 in **4 of
5 day-0 units** at 37.90–551.26 pg/mL against a 5 pg/mL limit, median 237 pg/mL.
The difference between the analytes at baseline is **constitutive versus
induced**, which is a different fact from a difference in induction timing.

The repair keeps the manuscript's wording **verbatim and quoted**, because
`data/` is immutable evidence and the row is the record of what it says, and
narrows the claim the row makes about it: `status` moves `verified` →
`verified_by_source_file`, and the `notes` column carries the full supersession
note. The `evidence_id`, `source_file`, `location` and the detection limits are
unchanged, because `config/coupling_adjudication.tsv` (rows `S_DECIDUALIZED` and
`S_PRL`), `OM-002`, `tests/unit/test_prl_igfbp1_axis.py` and
`tests/unit/test_observation_map.py` all cite this id and two of them read the
detection limits off this row.

**Nothing downstream moves.** `OM-002` stays `active_diagnostic` with its
comparator, supporting fraction and weight untouched, and its censoring note now
points at the correction instead of saying the conflict is unresolved. **No
IGFBP-1 ON/OFF binarisation threshold is chosen here** —
`docs/prl_igfbp1_temporal_axis.md` §5 forbids choosing one from the same series
that serves as the diagnostic, and that prohibition is the reason this remains an
open question rather than a rule change.

The conflict was **not discovered here**. `OM-002`'s censoring note,
`docs/prl_igfbp1_temporal_axis.md` §§3–4, `docs/v05_u2_gate3a_blocked.md` and
`docs/collaborator_metadata_2026-07-29.md` item 3 have recorded it since Gate 3a,
which chose to record rather than resolve it. This unit resolves it in the
register only.

## 4. What remains unresolved

1. **No PRL/IGFBP1 de-aliasing rule is licensed, and the barrier is not purely
   evidential.** The *structural* half of that claim holds: de-aliasing needs no
   twentieth free variable. Enumerated here over every single-free-input form of
   `S_IGFBP1 = S_DECIDUALIZED and [not] X`, **16** both take 35 → 36 distinct
   functions with the pair split *and* survive `OM-002`.

   But `MODEL_IMPLICATIONS.md` §3's named witness,
   `S_IGFBP1 = S_DECIDUALIZED and not IN_XAV939`, **is refuted by the observation
   layer and must not be quoted as the clean candidate.** `IN_XAV939` is `true`
   in `hormone_treated_maternal`, so that rule forces `S_IGFBP1` **OFF** in the
   one arm where the measured IGFBP-1 signal rises: evaluated on the committed
   network, `S_IGFBP1` is 0 in hormone-treated and 0 in basal, so `OM-002`'s
   directional expectation — `P(S_IGFBP1=ON | hormone_treated) >
   P(S_IGFBP1=ON | basal_maternal)` — **fails**. The observation-compatible
   polarity is the opposite one, `and IN_XAV939`; the package picked the wrong
   sign.

   **And that generalises**, which is the part worth carrying. Those 16 were then
   checked against every committed arm, maternal and challenge:

   - **10** condition on an input **no arm ever turns on** — `IN_HCG`,
     `IN_ER_STRESS_EXCESS`, `IN_HAND2_LOSS`, `IN_HIGH_ECM_STIFFNESS`,
     `IN_EMBRYO_CONDITIONED_SIGNAL`, `E_KLF12_HIGH` and the four `X_*` relays. The
     split exists only in states no arm visits, so it buys no design-class gain
     and nothing could ever measure it.
   - **2** — `and IN_E2` and `and IN_XAV939` — condition on an agent inside the
     four-agent bundle. `config/protocols.yaml` records that the design has no
     single-agent arm and that no effect may be attributed to MPA, cAMP or XAV939
     individually, and `docs/design_alias/node_design_classes.tsv` puts all four
     plus `S_PKA` in **one design-equivalence class**. They are observation-
     compatible precisely because they co-vary with everything else.
   - **4** condition on the challenge inputs (`IN_ANTI_SPP1`,
     `IN_EMBRYO_CONTACT`, `IN_EMBRYO_PRESENT`, `IN_TROPHECTODERM_COMPETENT`), i.e.
     they make a maternal stromal marker depend on the embryo. Nothing licenses
     that, and it would be a second readout-to-mechanism leak in the other
     direction.

   **No witness is both observation-compatible and identifiable under the existing
   design.** That is stronger than "the barrier is evidential", and it points at
   the same remedy as §1: a single-agent dropout arm.

   The evidential barrier is real too, and independent of the structural one:
   every discriminating perturbation in the corpus uses an agent with no ADOC
   counterpart (a FOXO1 activity inhibitor, insulin, TGFβ1), the FOXO1→PRL sign is
   unresolved across primary systems (§3.1 item 5), and Lynch 2009 makes
   `S_PRL = f(S_FOXO1)` **positively wrong** — FOXO1A alone is inert on the
   decidual PRL enhancer and needs a HOX partner that the model does not contain. Retis-Resendiz 2024 is the one candidate that is structurally
   adequate and it fails on evidence; it is **not** registered here.
   **The ADOC day-3/day-6 series must not be used to infer the rule**, and neither
   may temporal ordering alone — `docs/prl_igfbp1_temporal_axis.md` forbids
   fitting a rate to an onset ordering, and the magnitude gap (83× at day 3) plus
   the 2× difference in detection limits means a detection-fraction ordering
   cannot establish a kinetic one.
2. **Readout leakage must be repaired *before* any de-aliasing.**
   `OUT_STROMAL_DECIDUALIZED = S_DECIDUALIZED and S_PRL and S_IGFBP1`, and the two
   marker conjuncts **change the output in 0 of 524,288 assignments** — i.e. they
   are absorbed in *all* of them. Verified in this session:
   `OUT_STROMAL_DECIDUALIZED` is exactly `S_DECIDUALIZED`. (`MODEL_IMPLICATIONS.md`
   §2.3 and an earlier draft of this document both wrote "absorbed in 0 of
   524,288", which states the opposite of the result in the same sentence.) The alias
   is what makes the conjunction algebraically harmless. **The instant `S_PRL` and
   `S_IGFBP1` get independent rules, two calibration readouts become causal gates
   on the reported stromal phenotype `OUT_STROMAL_DECIDUALIZED`.** This sequencing
   constraint is recorded here and deliberately **not** acted on: no executable
   rule is repaired in this increment.

   *Corrected 2026-09-23 in U5b (`docs/v05_u5b_readout_repair.md`), which carries
   the repair out.* This sentence previously ended "causal gates **on adhesion
   competence**", and that overstated the blast radius. `OUT_STROMAL_DECIDUALIZED`
   is a leaf — no rule reads it — and `OUT_MATERNAL_ADHESION_COMPETENCE` reads
   `S_DECIDUALIZED` directly, so de-aliasing the markers would have gated the
   reported phenotype and nothing downstream of it. The sequencing constraint the
   sentence supports is unaffected and **stands**: the markers must not gate a
   reported phenotype, so the readout is repaired first. Only the stated
   consequence was wrong.
3. **`S_BMP2 ≡ S_PGR` is falsified with no replacement.** Li 2007 shows added
   BMP2 further stimulates differentiation on top of a complete hormone mixture,
   which a node fully determined by progestin could not do — and the identity is
   exact, verified by enumeration. Li supplies no replacement (no progestin-free
   human arm; mouse siRNA in mouse cells), and the obvious alternative lever is
   retracted. This is the highest-value structural question the corpus leaves
   open, and it is an **evidence-acquisition** problem.
4. **The collapsed integrin rule is diagnosed, not repaired.**
   `E_ITGAVB3_5 = E_LIF or E_HBEGF or E_SECRETORY_STATE` reduces to exactly
   `E_SECRETORY_STATE` — verified by enumeration — because `E_HBEGF` is itself
   exactly `E_SECRETORY_STATE`. Deleting the third disjunct is **Boolean-identical
   to the committed rule** and is therefore not a fix; the alias lives upstream.
   Separately, **Paule 2012 does not support a generic `PC6 → E_ITGAVB3_5` edge**:
   PC6 knockdown reduced surface αVβ5 while **αVβ3 did not significantly differ**,
   and the node merges the two. Splitting β3 from β5 is a prerequisite for using
   Paule at all. Neither the split nor the alias repair happens here.
5. **`E_MUC1_BARRIER` is contradicted and deliberately deferred.** The licensed
   repair is a **split** of abundance from local, contact-dependent barrier
   clearance — not a sign flip — and `GAP-004`/`GAP-011` already hold that open
   action. MUC16 may be the cleaner anti-adhesive candidate (Gipson 2008), which
   makes the repair a larger design question than a rule edit. Deferred to a later

## 7. Guards, and which of them changed meaning

Four existing guards were re-pinned. Three are value changes; one is a genuine
rescope and is flagged as such.

| guard | change | kind |
|---|---|---|
| `test_curation_increment_08_blocked_sources.test_the_disposition_tally_is_derived_and_the_documents_match_it` | tally `1/3/5/4` → `1/2/6/4`, and the four document fragments with it | value |
| `test_pdf_manifest.test_manifest_dois_that_are_cited_resolve_in_the_bibliography` | `46` → `60` (14 registered rows added; `PDF-059` deliberately not counted) | value |
| `test_pdf_manifest.test_files_recorded_as_not_versions_of_record_stay_countable` | pinned set `{PDF-048}` → `{PDF-048, PDF-054}`, on inspection of the file, which is the deliberate act that pin asks for | value |
| `test_v05_u4b_candidate_closure.test_an_activity_node_is_not_given_a_determinant_by_abundance` | `S_FOXO1` removed from the swept tuple | **semantic** |

**The semantic one, stated plainly.** That test pins external-review correction
B2: *an activity node is not given a determinant by abundance*. Removing
`S_FOXO1` from its list is **not** a relaxation of B2 — Labied's determinant is a
localisation measurement, which is the variable the node names, so B2's rule is
*satisfied*. But a list of names cannot distinguish "cleared (2) on a localisation
determinant" from "someone put the row back to (4) on abundance evidence", which
is the exact drift B2 was written to catch. So the row is not simply dropped: a
new sibling test,
`test_the_candidate_that_left_the_b2_set_cleared_it_on_a_localisation_read`,
asserts positively that `S_FOXO1` now reads `5(4)_delta_and_alias`, that it cites
`PIF-001`, and that `PIF-001`'s own `proximal_measurement` names the localisation
readout — immunofluorescence, separated fractions, and abundance readouts
reported *separately*. That is the same shape as the `E_SLEX_SURFACE` check in
`test_curation_increment_08_blocked_sources`, which pins "flow cytometry" and
"not a transcript proxy" rather than trusting a verdict.

Two structural repairs were made to the same module while it was open.
`LEDGER_PREFIXES` is no longer a hand-written tuple: it is derived from a new
`LEDGERS` mapping of prefix to file, and the two verdict-agreement loops
(`test_the_ledger_and_the_dispositions_table_agree_field_by_field`,
`test_a_stale_ledger_verdict_is_always_covered_by_a_later_one`) read that same
mapping. A prefix can no longer be admitted without its ledger also entering the
loops — the drift the previous hard-coded pair invited every time a ledger was
added.

`tests/unit/test_prl_igfbp1_foxo1_unit.py` is new: end-to-end registration across
**three** of the four registration artifacts — `references.tsv`,
`pdf_manifest.tsv` and the new ledger. It does **not** read
`citation_verification.tsv`; that file is guarded by
`test_citation_integrity.test_cache_covers_every_reference`, which fails until
`pixi run verify-citations` has covered every reference, so the fourth artifact is
protected elsewhere rather than here. Also: the machine-readable refusal of the
retracted paper, the author-manuscript flag, the no-PDF and
no-transport-archive rules, the `SOFIA-STR-001` supersession, and the constraint
that only `PIF-001` claims a disposition. Its "nothing executable moved" guard is deliberately **not** a
`git status` check — that version is inert on a clean tree, which is the failure
shape this repository keeps finding. The executable invariant is carried where it
binds against the committed file: `NoExecutableChangeTest` and
`NoMechanismChangeTest` pin 19 free variables and 35 distinct Boolean functions.
