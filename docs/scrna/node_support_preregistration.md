# Pre-registration: does the transcriptome support v0.4's nodes, compartments and Boolean identities?

Written **before any expression value for any audited gene was read** from the
deposited object. Committed on its own, as the first commit of this unit, so the
ordering is checkable in `git log` rather than asserted. The only single-cell
numbers that existed when this was written are the ones already committed at
`58f0fba`: the cell/sample/population counts in `docs/scrna_observability.md`,
and the KLF12 per-unit table in `docs/scrna/klf12_raw_umi_evidence.tsv`.

The owner's scope for this unit puts **all 27** RNA-assessable nodes through
node support, `E_KLF12_HIGH` included. Its per-unit values were therefore known
before this design existed, so its node-support row is recorded `not_blind` and
licenses nothing on its own. The KLF12 *prior* question is closed
(`docs/scrna/klf12_prior_finding.md`): no prior was activated, and nothing here
reopens it. `E_KLF12_HIGH` is a class of one and is not alias-tested.

Baseline: `adoc-literature-superset-v0.4.0` at `58f0fba`. Asset: `EXT-SCRNA-001`
(`E-MTAB-15842/seurat_object.rds`, sha256
`504a674db978779b03d02a73224ee425ec46efbf411c148b0903ceef15389a1a`,
2,877,048,290 bytes, 78,724 x 28,212).

## 0. What this unit is, and what it is not

It is **model refinement**: an audit of whether the transcriptomic evidence
supports the biological entities v0.4's nodes represent, their compartment
assignment, and the Boolean identities that force distinct states to move
together. It is **audit-only**. `network.bnd`, the condition `.cfg` files, the
calibration policy, the interface relays and the Stage-3 frozen design are not
edited in this unit. Gate 3b stays closed.

Some of these measurements were used to construct v0.4. That is admissible for
refinement and is recorded per row as `construction`; nothing produced here may
later be cited as independent validation of a rule it helped write.

## 1. Unit, compartment, and what is forbidden

**Biological unit: donor. There are three** (`SZ09`, `SZ10`, `SZ25`).
`config/preprocessing_contract.yaml` declares
`donor_is_independent_unit_for_sc_rna: true` and `config/personalization.yaml`
declares `independent_unit: donor`; neither is relaxed here. The primary
analysis grid is **donor x condition x primary compartment = 3 x 2 x 2 = 12
units**. `myofibroblast` adds six further compartment-units, so eighteen are
written to the artifact and twelve carry the audit.

Cells quantify *within-unit measurement precision* and are never biological
replicates. **No inferential statistic is computed across donors.** Donor
agreement is reported as 3/3, 2/3, 1/3, never as a p-value or an interval.

**Compartments — a complete, condition-independent partition of all 28,212
cells**, fixed here before any value is read:

| compartment | `celltype_v2` labels | cells | CNT | HT |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `epithelium` | Luminal-like epithelium, Cycling epithelium, HT epithelium I, HT epithelium II | 20,441 | 13,735 | 6,706 |
| `stroma` | Stroma, HT stroma | 7,406 | 4,742 | 2,664 |
| `myofibroblast` | Myofibroblast | 365 | 282 | 83 |

The union is the point. Every individual `celltype_v2` label is 80-99% one
condition, so a label-level CNT-vs-HT contrast is close to contrasting a
variable with itself; both primary compartments are well populated in both arms,
so a compartment-level contrast is not. The partition is a *definition*, and its
condition balance is a recorded diagnostic rather than an assumption.

`myofibroblast` is reported separately and is **excluded from the stromal
primary** (365 cells total across six units, and it is not the decidualizing
compartment). Including it is a declared sensitivity, decided here, not after
seeing results.

**Forbidden in this unit:** stratifying by an individual `celltype_v2` label for
any condition contrast; reconstructing `celltype_general`, which bakes treatment
group into the label; re-clustering, re-filtering or re-annotating the object;
and adding, substituting or curating any measurement feature (see §2).

## 2. Measurements: the frozen feature lists, and nothing else

Every gene used is taken verbatim from `measurement_features` in
`config/scrna_observation_map.tsv` as committed at `58f0fba`. **No feature is
added, substituted, dropped for convenience, or newly curated in this unit.**
Where the map records `not_observable` (32 nodes, including `E_DAG1_CLEAVED`,
`E_ANXA2_ACTIN` and `OUT_STROMAL_DECIDUALIZED`), nothing is measured and nothing
is inferred. That list was fixed before this analysis existed, and treating it as
frozen input is what keeps the audit from becoming a gene hunt.

The map is generated by `src/dt_adoc/scrna/observability.py` and is **not**
hand-edited here. This unit's classifications are written to their own derived
table.

### 2.1 Two exclusion rules, stated as rules rather than as a list

Both apply **only to the within-class concordance test** (§5). Node-support
(§4) uses the full committed feature list.

**R1 — same-class self-measurement.** In a within-class test, a composite
member's program excludes every gene that is the *entire* measurement of another
member of the same class. Otherwise the test asks whether a member agrees with
itself. Applying R1 mechanically:

| class | node | program after R1 | removed, because it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| AC03 | `E_PGR` | `PGR;GREB1;HOXA10;HOXA11` | `IHH` = all of `E_IHH` |
| AC02 | `S_DECIDUALIZED` | `FOXO1;WNT4;LEFTY2` | `PRL` = `S_PRL`, `IGFBP1` = `S_IGFBP1` |
| AC01 | `E_SECRETORY_STATE` | `CEACAM5;CEACAM6;DPP4;SCGB1D2;SCGB2A1` | `PAEP` = `E_PAEP`, `SPP1` = `E_SPP1_EXPR` |

**R2 — recorded direction-uninterpretability.** A gene is excluded from a node's
*direction vote* when the observation map's own `claim_ceiling` records that its
transcript direction does not track the node's state. R2 is a **reading of text
committed before this unit, not a decidable predicate over that text.** An
earlier draft said it applied "mechanically", which it cannot: the map has no
machine-readable direction field, only prose. What makes the reading honest is
that it is fixed here, the exclusions are enumerated, and the licensing sentence
for each is quoted, so a reviewer can disagree with the reading on the evidence
instead of reconstructing it. The distinction drawn is between a ceiling on
*level* — "TF TRANSCRIPT IS NOT TF ACTIVITY", which `S_NR2F2`, `S_HAND2` and
`S_TAZ_ECM` also carry, and which does not stop a direction being read — and a
ceiling on *direction*. Exactly two rows carry the second:

- `PGR` from `E_PGR` and `S_PGR` — *"in endometrium epithelial PGR falls exactly
  when progesterone signalling is highest, so the transcript can move opposite
  to the state."*
- `STAT3` from `E_GP130_STAT3` — *"STAT3 TRANSCRIPT IS A POOR PROXY FOR
  PHOSPHO-STAT3; only a downstream target program is defensible."*

Both rules are derived from text committed before this unit. R1 is mechanical
and its test pins the rule and recomputes its output. R2 is the reading above,
and its test pins the exclusion set **together with the licensing sentence for
each**, so changing either the reading or the committed text it rests on is a
visible diff rather than a silent retune.

## 3. Layer, and the limits of the layer — binding

**Primary layer: the deposited `counts` layer, which is decontX-corrected and is
not raw UMIs.** Its values are non-integer. Any method assuming integer counts is
misapplied on it, and no such method is used here.

### 3.1 What the zero/non-zero invariance evidence actually covers

`docs/scrna/klf12_prior_finding.md` states that decontX "never zeroes a detected
gene and never creates one, which is why both layers report the identical zero
fraction to twelve places." The supporting artifact is
`docs/scrna/klf12_raw_umi_findings.json`, key `cross_layer_corrected_vs_raw`:

```
n_cells 20441   both_positive 3429   both_zero 17012
corrected_positive_raw_zero 0        raw_positive_corrected_zero 0
```

**That is one gene (KLF12), in the epithelial compartment only, over 20,441 of
28,212 cells — one row of a 78,724-row matrix.** It is not a global result, not
whole-matrix, and not hash-bound. It does not cover the stroma, and it does not
cover any gene audited here.

**Consequences, binding on the implementation.**

1. This unit **does not claim to re-verify** raw-versus-corrected detection
   invariance. The raw per-sample matrices are not on local disk (the KLF12 unit
   fetched and removed them), and invariance cannot be checked on the corrected
   layer against itself. An earlier draft of this design proposed exactly that
   and it is void.
2. Every absence call made on the corrected layer alone is recorded
   **`provisional_corrected_layer_only`**, in the artifact and in every sentence
   that uses it.
3. **No structural conclusion may rest on a provisional absence.** If an absence
   call is load-bearing for a proposed v0.5 change, the audit states the change
   as *conditional on raw-matrix confirmation* and names the confirmation step —
   fetching the accession's per-sample `filtered_feature_bc_matrix` files (~3 GB)
   and re-deriving detection by barcode join, the route `klf12_raw_umi.py`
   already implements. It does not assert the change.
4. A conclusion that rests on **presence**, or on **divergence between two
   members measured on the same layer**, is not affected by this and is not
   marked provisional: both are comparisons within one layer.

### 3.2 The correction is condition-entangled

decontX is cluster-dependent and the clusters are near-deterministic in
condition. So the corrected layer may itself carry condition-associated
artifacts. This is a second, independent reason CNT-vs-HT magnitudes on this
layer are reported with the caveat attached, and it applies to presence as well
as absence. It is recorded here rather than discovered later.

## 4. Node support: numeric criteria, fixed before extraction

Primary quantity: **pseudobulk fraction** `f = (sum of the gene's corrected
values) / (sum of all corrected values)` over the cells of one compartment-unit.
Ratio-form, so it is not driven by the known depth outlier (`SZ25TE_HT` median
raw depth 68,954 against 14,503-17,079 for the other five units). Secondary:
**detection fraction** `d` = share of cells with a non-zero value.

### 4.1 The four numeric constants, and where each comes from

**Prevalence threshold, theta = 1% of cells.** The question a node asks is
whether its gene is expressed by a *population* of the compartment, not by a
handful of cells. At 1%, a compartment-unit of 2,000-5,000 cells still contains
20-50 expressing cells. Below it, the gene is absent as a compartment-level
program even if isolated cells carry a transcript. **This is a stated convention,
not a measured biological constant**, and it is the only one of the four that is
conventional rather than derived.

**Minimum cells, n_min = 300.** Derived from theta, not chosen. With zero
detections in n cells the exact one-sided 95% upper bound on the within-unit
cell-level detection rate is `1 - 0.05^(1/n)`. The smallest integer at which that
bound falls below theta = 1% is **n = 299** (0.009969; n = 298 gives 0.010002).
n_min is set at **300**, the round number immediately above it, giving a bound of
0.009936. It is theta's inverse image and moves only if theta moves.

**Minimum exposure, E_min = 1e7 corrected transcript-equivalents** per
compartment-unit. Derived: with zero observed, the 95% bound on the pseudobulk
fraction is `3/E`. The weakest pattern theta admits is 1% of cells carrying one
transcript each, i.e. `f ~ 0.01 / 15,000 ~ 6.7e-7` at the observed median depth.
E_min = 1e7 gives a bound of 3e-7, roughly 2.2x below that — so at E_min, zero
detection excludes even the weakest expression the prevalence threshold allows.
Expected to be satisfiable in the epithelium, on committed numbers. `E` is an
exposure on the **corrected** layer, so it is the corrected median depth that
enters it and not the raw one. `docs/scrna/klf12_state_evidence.tsv` gives the
six epithelial units as 3,909 / 2,537 / 4,143 / 2,765 / 5,683 / 1,404 cells at
corrected median depths of 12,936 / 13,511 / 11,759 / 11,827 / 11,692 / 61,403,
i.e. **3.3e7 to 8.6e7** — every one above E_min with at least three-fold of
margin. The tightest is `SZ10TE_HT` at ~3.3e7. The *smallest* unit by cell count
is `SZ25TE_HT` at 1,404 cells, which is nonetheless the largest exposure because
it is the depth outlier. (An earlier draft named 2,537 cells as the smallest
epithelial unit and multiplied it by a raw-layer depth; both were wrong, and
neither changed the conclusion. Section 11.) The stromal units' sizes are
committed nowhere and are **not** predicted here: whether they clear E_min is an
outcome of this audit, recorded per unit, not an assumption of it. E_min is
**not** expected to be satisfiable by `myofibroblast`, which is one reason that
compartment is not primary.

**Two positive controls, with two different jobs and two different failure
modes.** An earlier draft wrote one: a 50% detection floor on `EPCAM`
(epithelium) and `COL1A1` (stroma), justified by the claim that 50% "sits well
below their expected rate at this depth". That claim is true of `EPCAM` and
**false of `COL1A1`**, and a number committed before this unit says so.
`docs/scrna/klf12_state_findings.json`, key `marker_detection`, reports pooled
corrected-layer detection of `EPCAM` at **98.6%** inside the epithelial
population and `COL1A1` at **50.6%** outside it. A floor at 50% is headroom for
one and a coin flip for the other. Left as written it would have failed in an
unknown number of stromal units, and because `not_detected_with_power` requires
3/3 donors, a single failing unit would have removed the entire stromal arm —
AC02 whole, and the stromal half of AC03 — for a reason with nothing to do with
the biology under test. Corrected here, before extraction; see Section 11.

The single control conflated two questions its own sentence named: "a unit's
capture is degraded **or** the compartment assignment is wrong". They are
separated.

**C1 — detection integrity.** The compartment's high-prevalence transcript
detected in **>= 50%** of the unit's cells: `EPCAM` for `epithelium`, `VIM` for
`stroma`. Committed pooled values are 98.6% and 97.4%, so 50% is here what the
original sentence claimed and was not — a floor with roughly two-fold headroom
in both compartments, which a unit clears unless its capture is degraded. Both
genes are already in the committed context-gene list (`CONTEXT_GENES` in
`src/dt_adoc/scrna/klf12_state.py`: EPCAM, COL1A1, VIM, PTPRC, PGR, PAEP, LIF);
neither is newly curated here.

**C2 — compartment specificity.** The lineage-discriminating transcript detected
at a **strictly higher rate in its own compartment than in the complementary
compartment of the same donor and condition**: `COL1A1` higher in `stroma` than
in `epithelium`, and `EPCAM` higher in `epithelium` than in `stroma`. This is a
sign comparison with **no free constant** — nothing is read off the data to set
it — and it is the half of the control that actually tests compartment
assignment, which an absolute detection rate never did. `VIM` cannot do this
job: 97.4% outside the epithelium against 48.2% inside it, mesenchymal but not
stromal. `COL1A1` can: 50.6% against 17.9%. Committed pooled margins are 2.8x
for `COL1A1` and 1.8x for `EPCAM`, so C2 is easy to pass — which is what a
control should be, and is why it is a sign test and not a magnitude one.

**The two failures are different findings and are never collapsed into one.**

- **C1 fails** — the unit cannot support an *absence*. Every absence call in that
  compartment-unit becomes `uninformative_integrity`, and no
  `not_detected_with_power` may be assembled from an arm containing it.
  Detection values in that unit are still reported and tagged `integrity_failed`,
  because a depressed `d` in a degraded unit is not evidence of sparseness.
- **C2 fails** — the unit's *compartment attribution* is unsafe and its power is
  untouched. Absence and presence calls stand, tagged
  `compartment_attribution_unsafe`. What is forbidden is any
  `challenge_compartment_assignment` consequence drawn from that unit, and any
  claim that a finding belongs to that compartment rather than to the object's
  labelling of it.

Collapsing these would hide the distinction that matters: "we could not see" and
"we may have been looking at the wrong cells" license different repairs, and only
the first is a power problem. Both are reported per unit rather than dropped.

### 4.2 Support labels

Assigned per node per condition arm, requiring the stated donor agreement:

- **`robustly_detected`** — `d >= 10%` in 3/3 donors. (10% is a stated
  convention: the level at which a substantial share of the compartment, rather
  than a minority, carries the transcript.)
- **`sparse_or_low`** — detected, but below 10% in at least one donor.
- **`not_detected_with_power`** — zero detections, **and** n >= 300, **and**
  E >= 1e7, **and** **C1** passes, **and** all of that in 3/3 donors. Always
  additionally tagged `provisional_corrected_layer_only` per §3.1.
- **`uninformative`** — fails n_min, E_min, or C1. The reason is recorded rather
  than merged: `uninformative_n`, `uninformative_exposure`,
  `uninformative_integrity`.
- **`not_RNA_assessable`** — the map records `not_observable`. Nothing measured.

**C2 is not a support label and never produces one.** It is a separate recorded
axis on the compartment-unit, and a C2 failure changes what a finding may be
*attributed to*, not how much power there was to find it.

`not_detected_with_power` is the only label that licenses a `challenge_*`
consequence, and it never licenses one on its own where §3.1(3) applies.

## 5. The alias test, and what counts as movement

### 5.1 What v0.4 actually predicts

All five AC03 members reduce to `IN_PROGESTIN and not IN_MIFEPRISTONE`; AC01,
AC02 and AC04 reduce through `E_SECRETORY_STATE = IN_E2 and E_PGR` and
`S_DECIDUALIZED`. The protocol encoding of the deposited contrast
(`conditions/hormone_treated_maternal.cfg`) sets `IN_E2`, `IN_PROGESTIN`,
`IN_cAMP` and `IN_XAV939` together. So v0.4's prediction is:

> the entire measurable content of AC01, AC02, AC03 and AC04 is **one binary
> switch**, flipping OFF->ON with the cocktail, identically in all three donors.

The model's own contrast is bundled in exactly the way the experiment is, so the
bundled cocktail is a fit rather than a limitation. **The tested proposition is
always "the cocktail moves these together", never "E2 causes X" or "MPA causes
X".** No component-specific causal claim is admissible from this design, and
`config/protocols.yaml` already records that the design has no single-agent arm.

### 5.2 Precomputed expectations — Appendix A

Appendix A gives the v0.4 expected CNT->HT direction for **every** RNA-assessable
node, computed from `network.bnd` at `58f0fba` before any expression value was
read. Every supported/contradicted call in the audit must resolve against that
table, by node, and the table is not edited after extraction.

### 5.3 Material movement — sign consistency is not sufficient

With three donors, 3/3 sign agreement alone has null probability 0.25. Sign is
therefore necessary and never sufficient, and the artifact states that wherever
a 3/3 appears.

**Within-donor precision.** For each compartment-unit, cells are resampled with
replacement (B = 2,000, seed 20260917) to give a 95% interval on `f` and `d`.
This interval expresses *how precisely this donor's sampled cells measure that
donor*, and nothing else. It is **not** a biological-replicate interval, must
never be pooled across donors, and no across-donor statistic is computed from it.

**A member moves in a donor** only if **both** hold:

- **(a) separation** — the CNT and HT 95% bootstrap intervals of `f` do not
  overlap, and
- **(b) material size** — `|log2(f_HT / f_CNT)| >= 1`, i.e. at least two-fold,
  computed with a one-transcript-equivalent pseudocount on numerator and
  denominator so a zero arm is capped rather than infinite.

Two-fold is the conventional minimum effect treated as biologically meaningful in
expression analysis, and it is far above the numerical noise of a ratio over
>= 1e7 exposure. Stated as a convention. Criterion (a) alone would let an
arbitrarily tiny difference pass on cell count; criterion (b) alone would let
noise pass on a small unit; both are required.

Per donor a member is `moves_up`, `moves_down`, `no_material_move`, or
`uninformative`. Per arm it is then:

- **`concordant_with_alias`** — moves in the Appendix-A direction in 3/3 donors.
- **`divergent`** — `no_material_move` in 3/3, or moves *against* Appendix A in
  3/3.
- **`inconsistent`** — anything else. Not evidence for or against the alias.

### 5.4 Class verdicts, and a distinction that must not be collapsed

- **`alias_challenged`** — at least one member `concordant_with_alias` **and** at
  least one member `divergent`. This is the precise pattern v0.4 cannot
  represent: the members disagree, and the model has one variable for them.
- **`rule_challenged`** — all direction-callable members are `divergent`
  *together*. The members still agree with each other, so the alias is not
  falsified; what fails is the rule that says the cocktail switches them on.
- **`consistent_with_alias`** — all direction-callable members
  `concordant_with_alias`. This **does not confirm** the alias: agreeing on one
  bundled contrast is the weakest possible test of an identity, and a single
  contrast cannot distinguish "one variable" from "several variables that happen
  to move together here."
- **`uninformative`** — fewer than two direction-callable members.

A divergence tells us the alias is biologically inadequate. It does **not** tell
us the replacement rule, and the audit will not propose one as though it did.

### 5.5 Classes tested, in order

**AC03** (`E_PGR`, `E_IHH`, `S_PGR`, `S_NR2F2`, `S_BMP2`) first. It is **not**
the largest RNA-assessable class and an earlier draft said it was: AC01 is, with
seven assessable members of nine, against AC03's five of five. What puts AC03
first is a pre-recorded target:
`config/coupling_adjudication.tsv` currently records `E_PGR` and `S_PGR` as
**`UNIDENTIFIED`** with `what_would_change_it` = *"A compartment-resolved PGR
readout... The scRNA object now held could speak to the first."* This unit can
close a verdict the repository already left open, and it was left open before
this unit was designed.

Then **AC02**, **AC04**, **AC01**.

**AC05** (`E_PODXL`, `E_DAG1_FULL`, `E_MUC1_BARRIER`) is measured for node
support but **not** alias-tested: the adjudication records it as deliberately
`UNIDENTIFIED`, and `E_DAG1_FULL` is a genuine assay limit — the transcript
cannot distinguish intact from cleaved DAG1 — so a divergence test there would be
testing the assay, not the model.

### 5.6 Phenotype-inert members

`E_IHH`, `S_NR2F2`, `S_SPP1`, `E_HBEGF`, `E_MUC1_SLEX`, `E_SPP1_EXPR` and
`E_CD98_CD9` are phenotype-inert in v0.4: clamping any changes no `OUT_` node
across all 524,288 enumerated states. Findings on them are real and land as
**structural** consequences on the alias; they cannot reach any prediction
through v0.4, and the audit will not report an inert finding as if it moved one.

## 6. The asymmetry, stated as a binding rule

**Strong presence** of a direct transcript establishes **biological
availability** in that compartment and condition. It does **not** establish that
the corresponding Boolean state is ON. Transcript is not protein; receptor
transcript is not receptor activation; ligand transcript is not signalling;
a glycosyltransferase program is biosynthetic competence, never a measured
surface glycan. No `retain` verdict in this audit may be upgraded to
"node confirmed".

**Convincing absence** of a gene whose node *requires contemporaneous production
of that gene* is substantive negative evidence against that state being
active in the sampled ADOC condition — when it is reproducible in 3/3 donors, in
the correct compartment, above the exposure and control floors of §4.1, and not
explained by a known transient-timing argument. It is not to be dismissed as
"scRNA dropout": §4.1 exists precisely so that dismissal has to answer a number.

**But** absence at the sampled time does not prove the mechanism never acted
earlier, and absence cannot inform a node whose meaning is post-translational,
spatial or complex-level. For those nodes absence is recorded as
`cannot_inform_functional_state`, whatever the measurement shows.

## 7. Phase-A classification, assigned per node before extraction

Written to `docs/scrna/node_rna_interpretability.tsv`, derived from the frozen
map. Three independent axes:

- **measurement type** — `direct_transcript` | `gene_program` |
  `regulon_or_activity_proxy` | `abundance_proxy_only` | `not_RNA_assessable`
- **what presence can establish** — `direct_biological_availability` |
  `availability_only` | `supports_program_activity` | `weak_proxy_only` |
  `no_functional_inference`
- **what convincing absence can establish** —
  `challenges_contemporaneous_expression` | `challenges_constitutive_or_active_use`
  | `cannot_inform_functional_state` (post-translational / spatial /
  complex-level) | `inconclusive_sampling_time` (the relevant mechanism is
  transient and precedes sampling)

These are assigned from the map's `measurement_observability`,
`measurement_resolution` and `claim_ceiling` **before any expression value is
read**, and are not revised afterwards to fit a convenient pattern.

## 8. Model consequences — Phase D vocabulary

Per node: one support label from §4.2, and one consequence from

`retain` | `retain_state_uncertain` | `challenge_constitutive_or_active_use` |
`challenge_compartment_assignment` | `challenge_alias` |
`challenge_node_inclusion` | `no_model_consequence`

plus a **class-level** verdict from §5.4, because an alias finding belongs to the
class and not to one member.

**No consequence is applied to `network.bnd` in this unit.** The audit generates
evidence; candidate changes are proposals carrying, each, what supports them and
what is still missing.

## 9. The decision gate — two questions, kept apart

At the gate the audit answers these **separately**, and must not let the second
answer the first:

**Q1 — does the evidence justify a structural/topological revision?** Decided on
§5.4 class verdicts alone. An `alias_challenged` verdict resting on a presence or
divergence finding (not on a provisional absence, §3.1) is sufficient to propose
splitting that alias. **The absence of a rate layer is not a reason to refuse a
structural split.** A model that cannot express a distinction the data show
cannot represent them at any timescale, and giving a falsified alias its own
variable is a repair to what the model can *represent*.

**Q2 — are rates/kinetics identified?** Decided separately, on whether the
evidence constrains relative timing. `network.bnd` has **zero** rate
declarations, so v0.4 has no time semantics at all. The honest default answer is
no, and the consequence is a **limit on what a revised model may claim**, not a
veto on Q1: a v0.5 that splits an alias may claim the members are *distinct
states*, and may not claim anything about the *order* in which they change.

The two are different problems and are not to be merged. `S_PRL`/`S_IGFBP1` is a
**timing** falsifier (`OM-016`: ADOC's own ELISA shows IGFBP-1 precedes PRL) and
needs rates to repair fully; AC03 and AC04 aliasing is a **representational**
failure, and splitting those does not need rates. An earlier draft of this design
used the missing rate layer as a reason to defer all structural work, and that
conflated the two. Corrected here.

Parsimony is binding either way: the purpose is to remove artificial collapses
the evidence contradicts, not to reproduce a transcriptomic atlas in MaBoSS. A
proposal is admissible only if a named §5.4 verdict requires it.

## 10. Leakage and provenance rules, binding on the implementation

1. No model output, node occupancy, concordance value, attachment count or
   desired behaviour may enter the choice of compartment, layer, gene, threshold
   or criterion. `src/dt_adoc/scrna/node_support.py` must not import
   `dt_adoc.simulation`, `dt_adoc.concordance`, `dt_adoc.calibration`,
   `dt_adoc.scoring` or `dt_adoc.perturbations`, and must not read
   `docs/concordance/`. The test suite asserts the import boundary rather than
   trusting this paragraph.
2. These are the study's own cells. Evidence role stays `construction` or
   `same_dataset_internal` per the map; it may never become `held_out` or
   `external`, and nothing here is validation.
3. The per-cell table carries row-level sample metadata and is written **outside
   the repository**. Only per-unit aggregates are committed. `--from-per-cell`
   re-derives every committed number with no R and no 2.9 GB object, which is
   what makes the artifacts checkable by a reviewer.
4. `data/` is immutable. Nothing in this unit writes to it.
5. Every constant in §4.1 and §5.3 is pinned by a test, as are the C1/C2 control
   genes, the R1 rule and the R2 exclusion set with its licensing sentences.
   Changing one is a visible diff, not a silent retune.

## 11. Corrections made before extraction, and why each was made

This section exists so that the difference between *correcting a design* and
*moving a rule to fit a result* is checkable rather than promised. Every change
below was made **before any audited expression value was read**, on evidence that
was already committed at `58f0fba`, and the commit that carries this document is
the first of the unit. Nothing here is revisited afterwards.

1. **The stromal positive control was wrong, and its justification was
   contradicted by a committed number.** §4.1 required `COL1A1` detected in
   >= 50% of a stromal unit's cells and called 50% "well below their expected
   rate". `docs/scrna/klf12_state_findings.json` records pooled corrected-layer
   `COL1A1` detection outside the epithelium at **50.6%** — at the threshold, not
   below it. The control is now two controls: **C1** detection integrity
   (`EPCAM` 98.6% / `VIM` 97.4%, floor 50%) and **C2** compartment specificity
   (a sign comparison with no free constant). Their failures are **separate
   findings with separate consequences**: C1 removes absence *power*, C2 removes
   compartment *attribution*, and neither is reported as the other. This is the
   one correction that changes what the audit can conclude, and it is the one the
   repository owner adjudicated directly after an independent consultation could
   not be obtained (`aih consult`: Codex workspace out of credits — recorded as
   no opinion, not as agreement).
2. **`E_KLF12_HIGH` is in node support.** The owner's scope is all 27
   RNA-assessable nodes. Its row is tagged `not_blind` because its values
   preceded this design, it is not alias-tested, and the closed prior question
   stays closed.
3. **The exposure feasibility argument named the wrong unit and the wrong
   layer.** It claimed the smallest epithelial unit was 2,537 cells and
   multiplied it by a *raw* median depth to reach 4.2e7, while `E` is an exposure
   on the *corrected* layer. The smallest by cell count is `SZ25TE_HT` at 1,404;
   the tightest exposure is `SZ10TE_HT` at ~3.3e7. All six epithelial units clear
   E_min with >= 3x margin, so the conclusion was right and its support was not.
   E_min itself is unchanged.
4. **Appendix A mis-cited `baseline.cfg` on the interface relays.** They are not
   FALSE there; they carry mixed initial probabilities. Holding them FALSE is now
   declared as an evaluation choice, and `relay_invariance()` proves the audited
   expectations do not depend on it across all sixteen settings, instead of the
   document asserting it.
5. **AC03 was called the largest RNA-assessable class.** It is not; AC01 is,
   seven assessable members to five. The test order is unchanged — it never
   rested on size — and the real reason (a pre-recorded `UNIDENTIFIED`
   adjudication AC03 can close) is now the only one given.
6. **R2 was described as applying "mechanically".** It is a reading of committed
   prose, and no predicate over free text could be mechanical. The reading, its
   two exclusions and the sentence licensing each are now stated as such and
   pinned together by a test.
7. **The analysis grid said twelve units where eighteen are written.** Twelve
   primary compartment-units carry the audit; the six `myofibroblast` units are
   reported and never pooled into the stromal primary.

Corrections 2 to 7 are factual or editorial: none moves a threshold, a decision
rule or a verdict boundary. Correction 1 moves a criterion, which is why it is
argued from a committed number, adjudicated by the owner, and recorded here
rather than in a commit message.
