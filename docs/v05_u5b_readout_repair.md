**Status: complete (2026-09-23, `feat/v05-u5b-readout-repair`).** The first
mechanism-changing unit of the v0.5 line (`docs/v05_mechanistic_integration_plan.md`

## 1. The result

  S_DECIDUALIZED`, was `S_DECIDUALIZED and S_PRL and S_IGFBP1`. v0.5.1 is emitted

  19 free variables, AC01 = 9, decidual class = 6, eight alias classes, all

  `LE080` retired), the readout's depth 7 → 6, and **`S_PRL` and `S_IGFBP1`
  become phenotype-inert** (14 → 16): clamping either marker no longer switches

- **The transient moves, and was measured on a pre-registered paired design** —

- **`S_AKT_EXCESS` re-adjudicated on Kang 2022:** `EVIDENCE_UNVERIFIED` at §5(1)

## 3. Blast radius

`OUT_STROMAL_DECIDUALIZED` is read by **no** rule, and
`OUT_MATERNAL_ADHESION_COMPETENCE` reads `S_DECIDUALIZED` directly. So the
process restricted to every other node has the same generator in both versions
and therefore the same law. That is the exact statement. The paired run checks it
empirically (§4, expectation 2), and under common random numbers other nodes'
*realisations* can still differ once a trajectory reaches a state where the two
rules disagree — which is noise, not effect.

## 4. The transient — pre-registered, paired

**Pre-registration:** `docs/u5b_readout_repair/transient_preregistration.md`,
committed at `cdbd658` before v0.5.1 existed (`2db7fc9`). The only earlier
simulation was a v0.5.0-only sizing pilot.

**Design.** Four adhesion lineages with the projected continuation between phases,
ten deduplicated phase cells, each phase at its **declared** dimensionless
horizon. Replicate `r` runs both versions on the **same seed** (common random
numbers: a MaBoSS run with seed `s` and `N` trajectories is the union of
single-trajectory runs on `s … s+N−1`); blocks are disjoint between replicate
indices (`1 + r·20,000`) and between trajectory counts (`400,001 + r·2,000`).
`R = 20` at 20,000 trajectories, governing, and at 2,000 as a stability check.

**How the interval must be read — corrected at the owner's instruction
(2026-09-23), wording only.** The pre-registration computes a Bonferroni-adjusted
across-block t-interval and calls it one "at family-wise level 0.05". **That
phrase overstates it, and is withdrawn here without editing the frozen file.** A
coverage or family-wise level would require the replicate blocks to be
independent draws, and that is not established: the installed simulator seeds
trajectory `k` of a run as `s + k`, and consecutive per-trajectory seeds give a
**stratified**, not i.i.d., initial-state draw
(`docs/maboss_estimator_semantics.md`, addendum 2026-09-23). Disjoint blocks share
no seed; that does not make them independent. So what is reported is an
**empirical paired block-reproducibility interval**: across-block mean ± a
pre-declared multiplier (the Bonferroni-adjusted t-quantile) × across-block SD/√R.
It is a **stability screen**, not a confidence interval and not a Monte Carlo SE.
It carries no probabilistic coverage claim and no error rate, and no biological,
experimental or kinetic uncertainty. The computation, the multiplier, the
tolerance and the verdict table are **unchanged** from the pre-registration; only
the claim made about them is narrowed.

Read that way, a verdict is a statement about how wide this block-to-block
spread is against the tolerance, at this precision:

- `WITHIN_TOLERANCE` means **no detected transient change at this precision**,
  not "no change".
- `BEYOND_TOLERANCE` means the block-to-block band lies wholly outside ±0.005; the
  size is reported, and the measured magnitudes (table below: 17 to 93 times the
  tolerance) make that robust to any reasonable change of multiplier.

**Reading it.**

- **Seven hormone-exposed cells: `BEYOND_TOLERANCE`, all positive, +0.085 to
  +0.467 in occupancy units — 17 to 93 times the tolerance.** Removing the marker
  gate makes the readout switch on earlier: v0.5.0 waits for `S_PRL` and
  `S_IGFBP1` after `S_DECIDUALIZED`, v0.5.1 does not. The effect is **largest in
  the later lineage phases** (challenge phases read ≈0.01–0.02 on v0.5.0 and
  ≈0.09–0.26 on v0.5.1), because the projected continuation between phases keeps
  `S_DECIDUALIZED` but not the two markers (they are not in the
  `minimal_stable_joint` memory policy), so v0.5.0's readout had to re-wait for
  them at the start of every phase. At a declared horizon, v0.5.0's
  `OUT_STROMAL_DECIDUALIZED` was therefore mostly measuring marker re-activation
  lag, not decidualisation.
- **Three control cells: `EXACTLY_IDENTICAL`.** At 20,000 trajectories (governing) the two
  versions' runs are identical for every reported node at every tick in every block. At
  2,000 trajectories only the two estimands were compared, and every per-block paired
  difference is exactly zero; per-tick identity was not separately checked there.
- **Stability: all 20 primary intervals pass**, with the same verdict at 2,000
  and 20,000 trajectories (largest gap between the two estimates 0.0021,
  largest allowed 0.0145). The ratio
  SE₂ₖ / SE₂₀ₖ ranges 2.7–9.2, not the 3.2 of a
  1/√N law; no scaling law is claimed, as pre-registered.

**The pre-registered expectations.**

1. **Held.** Exact identity in all three cells where the rules never disagree —
   the direct check that common random numbers pair trajectory by trajectory.
2. **Not met at this precision, and not falsified.** Of 900
   blast-radius bands (every other reported node, ten cells, two estimands) at
   20,000 trajectories: 318 `EXACTLY_IDENTICAL`,
   530 `WITHIN_TOLERANCE`,
   52 `INDETERMINATE`, **0 `BEYOND_TOLERANCE`**.
   All 52 indeterminate bands straddle zero; 42 of them are the
   single-tick estimand, mostly in the late mifepristone and washout phases where
   the two versions' streams have diverged longest. The largest blast-radius
   mean is 0.0017 and the widest band end 0.0076.
   So nothing moved beyond the tolerance — the falsifying half of the
   expectation did not occur — but this design **cannot certify** ±0.005 for
   every node at the 900-band multiplier (5.16).
   The exact statement is the structural one in §3, not this screen.

**A pre-registration claim that was wrong, recorded rather than repaired.** §5 of
the pre-registration said the pilot SD put δ "about ten interval half-widths above
the resolution of this design". That held for the primary family (largest
primary half-width 0.0031) and did **not** hold for the blast-radius family, whose
900-way multiplier and single-tick estimand give half-widths up to ≈0.0067. The
pilot measured one node on one arm and could not have shown that. The design,
tolerance and verdict rules are unchanged; only the sizing sentence was wrong.

## 6. `S_AKT_EXCESS`, re-adjudicated

On evidence already registered — Kang 2022 (`REF-084`/`PDF-056`, `PIF-007`), re-read
against the same PDF for the three facts the verdict turns on — and recorded in
its own dated ledger, `u5b_s_akt_excess_2026-09-23.primary-verification.tsv`
(`U5B-001`), because writing it into `PIF-007` would backdate it.

Walking §5 in order:

- **(0)** The node is *pathologically excessive stromal AKT activity*. Kang
  observes exactly that: higher stromal p-AKT1 (Ser473) with total AKT1 unchanged
  in endometriosis eutopic endometrium, and CAPN7 gain of function raising
  p-AKT1 (Ser473) in primary HESC. Ser473 is an activation-site phosphorylation,
  so this is an activity readout, not abundance. Established, in a disease frame
  → node verdict `JUSTIFIED_SYSTEM_LIMITED`.
- **(0b)** No committed node represents AKT or PI3K activity.
- **(1)** **Satisfied** — which is what the row's own unblock condition asked for.
- **(2)** **Fails.** This repository reads (2) as a determinant of the named
  variable that ADOC varies (`S_FOXO1`: MPA and cAMP; `E_JAG1_NOTCH3`: the
  hormonal protocol). Kang's determinants are CAPN7 and the endometriosis state,
  and ADOC varies neither. Under the owner-ratified discriminator that is
  `EXTERNAL` → **`RETAIN_CATALOG_ONLY`**.

**The plan predicted the disposition would not move, and the label does move.**
§5's table allows `EVIDENCE_UNVERIFIED` only for a failure at (1); once (1) clears
the label must change. What does not move is promotion: the encoding stays
`NONE_PROPOSED`, and none was constructed in order to be measured. Same shape as
`S_FOXO1` on 2026-09-22 — an evidence advance recorded as a later failing rung.

**Recorded for any later edge, not for this node:** neither Kang (no AKT
inhibitor or knockdown) nor Labied 2006 (LY294002, PI3K and not AKT-specific)
tests **AKT necessity** for FOXO1 exclusion. A future `S_AKT_EXCESS ⊣ S_FOXO1`
edge would need it, and `S_FOXO1` itself is catalogue-only at §5(4).
