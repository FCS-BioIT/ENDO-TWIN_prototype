# Scientific verification report — `endotwin-v0.3.0`

Generated by `pixi run endotwin-verification-report` from the committed
results. Do not edit by hand.

> **Computational verification is not biological validation.** Everything
> below checks that the implementation computes what the stated rules say it
> should, and that the conclusions survive the uncertainties that were
> examined. The model has **not** been calibrated against experimental data
> and **no** prediction has been evaluated prospectively. MaBoSS
> probabilities are simulated trajectory frequencies under stated rules,
> rates and initial conditions: not concentrations, not cell fractions, not
> clinical probabilities. MaBoSS time is not experimental time.

**Read the rest narrowly too.** It says the predicted *directions*
survive the rate sets that were sampled, within the stated range. It is
not a claim about rates outside that range, about magnitudes, or about
timing. The network is acyclic, so its fixed points cannot depend on
transition rates or on the initial state at all; what the sampling probes
is whether a finite phase reaches them.

## 5. Which competing hypotheses the planned perturbations separate

The model carries **5 hypothesis families**. A family is
separated when some comparison predicts opposite directions for its
variants in every rule scenario and under every sampled rate set.

**Funded** means the perturbed arm needs no reagent the node dictionary
marks as outside the funded protocol. That distinction matters: a family
separable only by an unfunded candidate is not resolved by the work plan
as it stands.

| Family | Variants | Separated by a funded arm? | Only by a candidate? | By a functional readout? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `CAM` | 2 | yes | no | yes |
| `FOX` | 2 | **no** | **yes** | yes |
| `INF` | 2 | yes | no | no |
| `Q1` | 2 | yes | no | yes |
| `Q2` | 3 | yes | no | yes |

**4 of 5 families are separated by an arm the work plan funds.**
Separable only by a candidate perturbation that is not in the funded protocol: `FOX`. The work plan as written does not decide it, so any prediction resting on that family stays conditional.

**These are model scenarios, not biological replicates.** A scenario is
one combination of competing rules, enumerated exhaustively on a computer.
It carries no donor, no chip and no measurement, and the count must never
be reported as a sample size. The biological unit is the donor.
